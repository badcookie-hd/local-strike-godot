# Asset Licenses

Local Strike uses original procedural geometry and effects together with the following selected CC0 files. Only assets used at runtime are included.

## Poly Haven - CC0

- `Concrete Floor Worn 001` by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers
  - Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001
  - Files: 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal, and ARM maps
- `Metal Plate` by Rob Tuytel
  - Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate
  - Files: 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal, and ARM maps

Poly Haven assets are published under the Creative Commons CC0 license:
https://polyhaven.com/license

## Kenney - Impact Sounds - CC0

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Included files: `footstep_concrete_000.ogg`, `impactGeneric_light_000.ogg`, `impactMetal_medium_000.ogg`, `impactGlass_light_000.ogg`, `impactGlass_heavy_000.ogg`
- The original package license is included at `assets/audio/kenney_impact/License.txt`.

Kenney assets are published under Creative Commons CC0:
https://kenney.nl/support

Quaternius packs were evaluated for the upgrade, but no Quaternius source files are checked in because their public host throttled the selective download and the project must remain importable without Blender. The current weapon and character geometry is original procedural geometry.

No Counter-Strike artwork, audio, maps, logos, or other proprietary assets are included.
